Kellytoy Mario

This is the Kellytoy Mario plush made back in early 2000s made by Kellytoy for the release of the Nintendo set. The first time this lil guy was made he had a manufacturing error where he was given black eyes, but later releases corrected it to give him blue eyes. These plushies were made at the carnivals, so they are prone to be cheaply made and have little to no quality control. But for some reason these little guys have gotten a bit pricey. More evidently on eBay where they can cost at least 20 dollars for a somewhat decent condition.
